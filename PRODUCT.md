# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People already abroad on a working holiday visa who need to find jobs and accommodation.

## Product Purpose

Help people on a working holiday visa find jobs and accommodation while abroad. The planned job experience includes an AI agent that finds vacancies, organizes their details, assesses whether it may apply, and sends applications within rules approved by the user.

## Positioning

The planned differentiator for jobs is an AI agent that finds and organizes vacancies and applies autonomously within user-approved rules. The accommodation experience and its differentiation remain open.

## Operating Context

Users are living abroad under a working holiday visa when they use the product. The job agent uses the user's CV, visa, location, availability, and salary requirements to assess where it may apply.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core focus: jobs and accommodation. The first planned job experience is for Australia.
- First-release job capabilities: functional AI discovery and organization of vacancies, assessment against approved rules in the backend, and application within those rules. The primary view shows available vacancies and application controls.
- Users can search and filter vacancies, select a filtered group for AI-assisted bulk application, trigger an AI application for one vacancy, or allow automatic application per vacancy within approved rules. They can pause the AI and adjust those rules.
- Open: additional countries, languages, job and accommodation categories, vacancy sources, search and application mechanisms, and the accommodation workflow.
- No additional product constraints have been decided yet.

## Evidence on Hand

The project has a responsive frontend preview with fictitious vacancies. It has no real vacancy data, live AI integration, confirmed application results, or verified brand assets. Future work should not present unverified listings, partnerships, successful applications, or live AI behavior as established facts.

## Product Principles

- Serve people already abroad on a working holiday visa.
- Make the agent's application actions and status clear in the core job workflow while keeping detailed rule checks in the backend.
- Keep job details and application outcomes tied to actual data and actions.
- Keep the accommodation experience within the product scope without inventing its workflow.
